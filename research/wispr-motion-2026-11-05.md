# Wispr homepage motion revisit — 5 November 2026

Reference: https://wisprflow.ai/ (homepage only). Read the full homepage structure and inspected live desktop views of the opening, sticky feature/personalisation chapters, review sequence, FAQ and closing section. No reference code or proprietary media was copied.

## Observed

- Live computed heading font: EB Garamond, 96px with 91.2px line-height at the observed desktop viewport. Body: Figtree.
- Spacious centered type, italic emphasis, a framed floating navigation, large rounded chapter boundaries and editorial cards.
- The review section occupies a long scroll region and uses a viewport-height sticky stage. Cards follow a diagonal path with translation, X/Z rotation and changing depth. The sequence contains both wide image/text cards and compact quotation cards.
- Personalisation pairs a sticky visual with a vertical reading sequence. Nearby inactive text has reduced emphasis. The final call to action uses very large expressive serif type.

## Wood N Work adaptation

- Use the same open-source font families, hosted locally with licences, while retaining white, beige and brown brand colours and the supplied logo.
- Build original word reveals, staggered entrances, image parallax, reading emphasis and a changing material composition.
- Use the diagonal scroll stage for clearly labelled design concepts and approved business strengths. No names, quotations, outcomes or customer reviews are fabricated.
- Keep the opening film free of overlays and replay controls. No essential navigation, form field or information depends on animation.
- Native scroll stays available. Reduced motion/no JavaScript/short screens use an ordinary story-card sequence, with all copy and links available.

Font sources: Google Fonts CSS API for EB Garamond and Figtree; licence notices from https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ebgaramond and https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/figtree . Fonts total roughly 90 KB compressed for the selected Latin subsets.
