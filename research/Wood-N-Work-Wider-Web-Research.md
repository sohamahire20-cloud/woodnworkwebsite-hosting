## What the placeholder actually showed

The successful rendered retrieval of https://woodnwork.in/ displayed a short launch notice and Contact Us heading, copyright 2025, GoDaddy website-builder link, cookie notice and one background-media URL. There was no usable portfolio, service detail, quote flow, physical location or structured business identity in the returned readable content. A copyright year is not evidence of company age. The placeholder's existence does not establish whether it is the intended production domain, who controls it or whether the Instagram owner is its registrant.

The initial standard web tool could not open either https://woodnwork.in/ or https://www.woodnwork.in/. A later Apify web-fetch returned a TLS verification error. Since one rendered retrieval succeeded and another failed, report this as inconsistent access requiring a prelaunch technical check, not as proof that the domain is permanently unavailable.

## Implications for the six-page website plan

These are research-led recommendations, not statements that the business already does them:

1. Establish identity immediately: consistent approved brand spelling, Nashik, correct phone and link to the supplied Instagram. Add verified physical address and actual service area when owner provides them.
2. The website should provide reliable depth beyond Instagram: clear kitchen/wardrobe options, real materials and finish details, manufacturing/process explanation, enquiry path and business background. Root's meeting analysis should determine final six-page allocation; wider Instagram offerings need not all become launch pages.
3. Explain the Mahavir relationship in plain language only after confirming legal/display name, relationship, history dates, offerings and correct contact. A parent's experience should not be represented as Wood N Work's operating age.
4. Treat factory-direct as a positioning claim requiring visual evidence: genuine workshop photos, production-stage detail and clarity about design, manufacture, installation and aftercare responsibilities. Avoid invented percentage savings or delivery guarantees.
5. Use real product/workshop images and owner-approved descriptions. Where projects are a later-phase objective, launch with properly labelled product/design examples; do not invent completed projects or client testimonials.
6. Maintain a clear enquiry flow: service type, location, approximate dimensions, budget range if approved, timeline and optional reference image. Phone/WhatsApp can provide the simplest launch conversion if owner confirms the number accepts WhatsApp and who responds.
7. Because exact-name search is ambiguous, page titles and business descriptions should combine the brand with Nashik and the actual category. Link the eventual domain from Instagram and align all approved listings. These are findability recommendations, not traffic/ranking promises.
8. Confirm domain ownership/access, canonical www/non-www, HTTPS certificate and redirects before launch. Do not assume the existing placeholder can be edited.
