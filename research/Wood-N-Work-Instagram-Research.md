- Dark brown or white rectangular logo overlays; thin line rules, generous white/cream areas; serif display headlines combined with plain sans-serif body copy.
- Spacious kitchens with islands, slatted/fluted cabinet or TV-unit details, warm ambient lighting, rounded wood furniture, stone worktops, plants, neutral upholstery.
- Heritage post mixes pencil-style illustrations of craftspeople with polished cabinet imagery; process post uses icons and a clear vertical supply-chain comparison.
- This is useful art direction, but imagery provenance is not established for all posts. The AI label on DeyiQpOTZxa makes a real-photo/inspiration distinction essential for future content management.
- No named client/project/location case study was observed. No written substantive customer review was found in the opened posts; emojis are engagement, not testimonials.

## Website recommendations arising from Instagram (for synthesis with meeting)
1. Frame the business as custom modular furniture with timber/material knowledge and direct manufacturing positioning.
2. Lead with kitchens and wardrobes if the meeting confirms these priorities; give secondary offerings compact cards rather than six separate service pages.
3. Use a quote/consultation path with phone and optional verified WhatsApp, not cart/checkout unless client later requests ecommerce.
4. Explain how enquiry, site measurement, design/material approval, manufacturing, installation and handover work, but confirm the exact steps and inclusions with client.
5. Keep legacy claim precise: “Built on a 35+ year timber/plywood legacy” only after parent history is approved, never 35+ years of this current brand by assumption.
6. Use real workshop, material and product photography when ready. Label renders/concepts as such. Client's deferred project section can be honored; Instagram offers no basis to invent case studies.
7. Establish one consistent brand spelling and phone format across website, Google business listing and social profiles.
8. Confirm workshop/showroom address, service radius, material brands/grades, warranty, quote process, timeline, after-sales, parent relationship, photography rights and WhatsApp availability before launch copy is finalized.
