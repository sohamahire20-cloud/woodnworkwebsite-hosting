# Wood N Work: research and six-page website plan

Prepared 1 November 2026 · Research and planning only

## Recommendation

Plan an enquiry-led website with exactly six main pages: **Home, About Us, Kitchens, Wardrobes, Plywood & Hardware, and Get in Touch**. Its job is to explain the offering, establish credible business identity and material expertise, and turn interest into useful enquiries saved in the backend and notified to the business on WhatsApp.

Keep the presentation light, warm and restrained: white backgrounds, beige surfaces, brown accents, concise content and one kitchen-shutter animation at the top of Home. Use one approved representative image per product/type at launch. Add project case studies and an AI chatbot in a later phase, following the meeting decisions.

The strongest opportunity is to connect three things clearly: **custom kitchens and wardrobes, a timber/plywood background, and the brand's stated factory-direct approach**. Each claim should have accurate wording and appropriate supporting evidence.

## 4. Positioning and intended audience

**Recommended positioning:** custom kitchens and wardrobes built around the customer's space, supported by material knowledge and the brand's factory-direct approach.

Suggested headline direction, subject to client approval:

> Kitchens and wardrobes, made for your space.

Suggested supporting line:

> Explore made-to-order solutions from Wood N Work in Nashik. Share your space, style and requirements with us.

Use factory-direct and heritage statements beside supporting explanations once approved. Avoid unsupported claims such as “Nashik's best,” guaranteed savings, guaranteed delivery dates, lifetime warranties or a specific number of completed homes.

**Primary audience, inferred from the meeting:** people planning a kitchen, wardrobe or home fit-out in the confirmed service area. Their questions are what can be made, how it fits their space, which materials are available, what happens next and how to request a quote.

**Secondary proposed audience:** people enquiring about plywood/hardware, plus designers or contractors if the owner confirms that these are intended customers. Do not make trade supply a major promise without confirming availability and terms.

The website should answer those practical questions with a small amount of useful content. It does not need a long luxury-brand manifesto.

## 5. The six-page plan

| Page | Proposed route | Main job | Primary action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home | `/` | Explain the business and direct visitors to the right offering | Share Your Requirement |
| About Us | `/about` | Establish identity, heritage and a credible process | Discuss Your Project |
| Kitchens | `/kitchens` | Show approved kitchen options and customization | Enquire About a Kitchen |
| Wardrobes | `/wardrobes` | Show approved wardrobe options and storage choices | Plan My Wardrobe |
| Plywood & Hardware | `/plywood-hardware` | Present the integrated Mahavir materials/display offering | Enquire About Materials |
| Get in Touch | `/contact` | Capture a useful enquiry and offer direct contact | Send Enquiry |

### Page 1 — Home

**Purpose:** make the business understandable immediately and move people toward the right enquiry.

Recommended section order:

1. Header with approved logo, six-page navigation and a clear enquiry button.
2. Hero with one approved kitchen visual, short headline, Nashik identity and the kitchen-shutter animation. Include “Share Your Requirement” and a quieter “Explore Kitchens” action.
3. Kitchens and Wardrobes as the two lead product cards, one image and one useful sentence each.
4. A compact Plywood & Hardware introduction explaining its relationship to Wood N Work once confirmed.
5. Three concise reasons to enquire: custom fit, material knowledge and direct manufacturing approach, using approved wording.
6. A simple proposed journey: discuss requirements → confirm design/materials → manufacture → install/handover. Validate the actual business process before publication.
7. A small “More made-to-order options” area for approved beds, TV units, doors and office/custom furniture. These lead to a preselected enquiry, preserving six pages.
8. Final enquiry invitation and footer with contact/social details.

**Inputs needed:** final logo, approved hero image, shutter reference, core product images and agreed business proposition.

**Acceptance:** the visitor can identify the offering, location and next step immediately; the page remains understandable without animation. No portfolio counts or unverified customer badges.

### Page 2 — About Us

**Purpose:** show who stands behind the brand and explain the timber/materials connection accurately.

Recommended sections:

1. Short introduction to Wood N Work and the approved Siddham Bhandari attribution.
2. A plain explanation of the relationship to the parent concern/Mahavir.
3. A short heritage timeline with a verified start year and correctly attributed experience.
4. Genuine team, workshop or materials photographs with concise captions where available.
5. A short explanation of customization and the actual design-to-handover process.
6. A relevant enquiry button.

**Inputs needed:** exact parent name, relationship, founder/team descriptions, approved dates, factory role/location and genuine photographs.

**Acceptance:** the reader can distinguish the current furniture brand from the older parent/materials business. If heritage documentation is pending, use a factual relationship description without a numerical age claim.

### Page 3 — Kitchens

**Purpose:** help a visitor describe the kitchen they need without overwhelming them with a catalogue.

Recommended sections:

1. One strong approved kitchen image and a concise proposition about fit, storage and daily use.
2. Client-confirmed kitchen types, each with one representative image and a short explanation. L-shaped, U-shaped, parallel, straight or island layouts are possible organizational examples only; the client must supply the actual range.
3. A compact guide to approved finishes, shutters, storage and hardware options. Show only materials/brands genuinely offered.
4. A short “What we handle” explanation covering the confirmed measurement, design, manufacture and installation scope.
5. Three or four approved FAQs addressing quotation, measurements, materials and process. Publish timelines and warranties only when confirmed.
6. “Enquire About a Kitchen,” opening Contact with Kitchen preselected.

**Inputs needed:** the promised kitchen-type list, one image per approved type, real specifications, scope and quotation process.

**Acceptance:** options are real and accurately labelled; no fictional package prices, stone-fabrication claim or automatic cost calculator. The image may be a labelled concept if installed-product photography is unavailable.

### Page 4 — Wardrobes

**Purpose:** help customers identify a suitable storage and access arrangement.

Recommended sections:

1. Approved wardrobe image and a short introduction focused on space and everyday storage.
2. Client-confirmed wardrobe types, one image per type. Hinged, sliding and walk-in are examples for discussion, not verified current products.
3. Optional internal organization choices such as hanging space, shelves, drawers and lofts, only where actually available.
4. Concise approved finish/hardware choices and customization limits.
5. Brief measurement and consultation guidance, plus a few useful FAQs.
6. “Plan My Wardrobe,” opening Contact with Wardrobe preselected.

**Inputs needed:** wardrobe-type list, images, configurations, materials and installation details.

**Acceptance:** the page explains choices in everyday language and collects an enquiry without promising that every inspiration image can be reproduced exactly.

### Page 5 — Plywood & Hardware

**Purpose:** satisfy the explicit Mahavir integration requirement and connect material selection with the furniture offering.

Recommended sections:

1. Accurate introduction to the parent/materials business, using the confirmed name and relationship.
2. One approved plywood-display photograph and one hardware-display photograph, or clearly labelled illustrative images until the physical display is ready.
3. A short list of confirmed product categories. Add brands, grades, finishes or specifications only after verification.
4. Explain how visitors can view samples or discuss materials. Publish a visit address and hours only when the facility is ready and details are approved.
5. A brief link back to Kitchens/Wardrobes so the materials page supports the overall business story.
6. “Enquire About Materials,” opening Contact with Plywood & Hardware preselected.

**Inputs needed:** exact Mahavir spelling, its business role, actual display readiness, product inventory, approved brand relationships and visit details.

**Acceptance:** the visitor knows whether they are requesting material advice, a showroom/display visit or a supply quotation. The site does not imply an online stock system, dealership status or checkout that the business has not confirmed.

### Page 6 — Get in Touch

**Purpose:** turn interest into a complete, actionable lead with a dependable delivery path.

Recommended layout: a short invitation and enquiry form alongside phone/WhatsApp/Instagram details. Add a map, street address, hours and visit instructions after verification.

| Field | Proposed behavior |
|---|---|
| Name | Required; recommended addition to the meeting fields |
| Requirement | Required: Kitchen, Wardrobe, Plywood & Hardware, Other Custom Furniture; allow a short clarification |
| Project location | Required city/locality; recommended addition for service-area assessment |
| Available space | Dimensions with unit selection, or “Need help measuring” |
| Additional details | Short free-text description |
| Phone number | Required, with validation |
| Email | Include as agreed; recommend optional unless the owner needs it on every lead |
| Desired timing | Date/range or “Flexible”; this records a preference, not a delivery promise |
| Budget | Owner-approved ranges plus “Not sure yet” |
| Contact/data-use acknowledgement | Brief explanation of how the enquiry will be used; optional marketing permission should be separate |

Keep the form compact, labelled and usable on mobile. Avoid forced account creation. Do not add file uploads at launch unless there is a clear business need and a supported handling process.

**Acceptance:** a valid submission creates one backend lead, shows an accurate success state and triggers the agreed notification workflow. Validation failures retain the visitor's input. A notification problem must not discard the saved enquiry.

## 6. Enquiries, WhatsApp and Instagram

### Separate the two WhatsApp functions

**Direct chat:** a WhatsApp button opens the verified business chat, optionally with a short product-specific message. The visitor still chooses to send it. This is useful for immediate contact, but does not by itself satisfy the meeting's automatic form-notification requirement.

**Automatic business notification:** form submission first saves the lead, then a server-side integration notifies the designated business recipient. Select a supported WhatsApp Business Platform integration/provider and confirm the sending account, receiving number, account readiness, applicable message templates, costs and delivery behavior before implementation. Do not promise this is available merely because a public WhatsApp number exists.

Meta's hosted documentation demonstrates approved template-message sending through an API; that particular Node SDK is archived, so it is evidence of the integration mechanism rather than a recommendation to adopt that library. Recheck current platform documentation during implementation. [Meta-hosted template-message reference](https://whatsapp.github.io/WhatsApp-Nodejs-SDK/api-reference/messages/template/)

Recommended lead flow:

1. Validate and save the enquiry with a unique reference and timestamp.
2. Show the visitor a success message only after saving succeeds.
3. Queue/send a concise business alert with the enquiry reference and useful requirement details.
4. Track notification success/failure, retry safely and provide an agreed fallback alert or admin visibility.
5. Let the assigned person follow up and record lead status.

Keep operational lead storage authoritative. Automatic alert delivery must be tested end to end, including failure cases. The customer-facing form should explain the intended contact use; no exact response-time promise should appear until someone owns it.

### Social integration

Link to the exact [Instagram profile](https://www.instagram.com/woodnwork.in/) and verified WhatsApp destination. Prefer a normal Instagram link over a heavy embedded feed at launch; it fits the minimal brief and avoids importing uncertain image labels or relying on third-party content to explain the offering.

## 7. Visual and interaction brief

- **Confirmed:** white backgrounds, brown/beige/white palette, light overall appearance and a kitchen-shutter animation.
- **Recommended:** warm product imagery, generous spacing, restrained wood/stone detail, short headlines and a highly readable body font. Reuse one approved logo consistently.
- **Typography direction:** a restrained serif or distinctive display face for headings paired with a simple sans-serif body face; select after logo approval.
- **Motion:** one clear shutter interaction, with click/tap/keyboard access where interactive, a static fallback and reduced-motion support. Avoid making content depend on scrolling through an animation.
- **Performance:** reserve the hero image's space, compress images and defer optional effects. Motion failure should leave a usable page. No embedded website video in launch scope.
- **Mobile:** clear menu, large controls, visible form labels, sensible input keyboards and contact buttons that do not obstruct content.
- **Language recommendation:** start with concise English copy and confirm whether Hindi/Marathi support is needed. The meeting's spoken language alone does not decide the website's language.

The reference site and exact shutter movement remain unresolved inputs. Do not claim that its framework has been studied or reproduce a guessed reference.

## 9. Launch scope, later work and validation

**Launch:** the six pages, approved product examples, concise company/heritage copy, one shutter animation, enquiry form with backend storage, agreed automatic WhatsApp alerts, direct contact/social links and basic search metadata using the approved Nashik identity.

**Later:** genuine project case studies when evidence is available; an AI chatbot based on approved business answers; expanded product detail pages if useful; additional language versions if requested. A later Projects page would change the six-page architecture unless an existing page is replaced.

**Separate deliverables:** logo redesign, marketing videos and campaigns. The meeting expresses urgency for them, but this request authorizes research and planning only.

Recommended validation before eventual launch:

- Confirm six main routes, working navigation and accurate preselected enquiry categories.
- Review every published claim, image label, parent-history statement and contact destination.
- Test a real form save and business WhatsApp alert; then simulate notification failure and duplicate submission.
- Check mobile layout, keyboard navigation, labels, contrast, reduced-motion behavior and static fallback.
- Confirm domain ownership, secure access, redirects, page titles and social previews.
- Review how the owner can inspect leads and update the small product catalogue.

For the six-page constraint, place contact/data-use information in an accessible footer panel initially; agree separately if a standalone policy route is needed. Do not silently expand the main sitemap.

Suggested measurement after launch: saved qualified enquiries, WhatsApp contact clicks, form abandonment and lead follow-up status. Counts and targets should be set after a baseline exists; no fabricated conversion forecast is included.
